## [IS7.1.1.a] Describe the basic structure of a plant and animal cell

Sub-strand: 7.1 Cells
Key inquiry question: How are plant and animal cells similar and different?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners observe labelled cell diagrams or prepared slides and compare cell structures using a table.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Communication and collaboration.
Values: Respect for evidence; Unity.

## [IS7.2.1.a] Explain energy changes in common appliances

Sub-strand: 7.2 Energy
Key inquiry question: What happens to energy when an appliance is used?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners identify the input and useful output energy of a torch, radio, kettle and fan, then discuss wasted energy.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Digital literacy.
Values: Responsibility; Environmental conservation.

## [IS7.3.1.a] Compare physical and chemical changes

Sub-strand: 7.3 Changes in matter
Key inquiry question: How can we tell whether a change creates a new substance?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners compare melting ice, dissolving salt, rusting iron and cooking an egg, using observations to classify each change.
Core competencies: Observation; Critical thinking and problem solving.
Values: Honesty in reporting observations; Safety.
