## Item 1: Sorting substances

Outcomes: IS8.1.1.a
Item type: Classification task
Question: Classify each of the following as an element or a compound and give a reason for any two: oxygen, carbon dioxide, iron, sodium chloride, sugar, copper.
Success criteria: Correctly classifies at least five of six substances; reasons refer to one kind of atom versus elements chemically combined.
Rubric: Exceeds expectations 6 correct with two clear reasons; Meets expectations 5 correct with one reason; Approaches expectations 3 to 4 correct; Below expectations fewer than 3.

## Item 2: Chemical symbols

Outcomes: IS8.1.1.b
Item type: Short answer
Question: Write the chemical symbols for hydrogen, carbon, sodium, magnesium, chlorine and calcium. Explain why the symbol for sodium is Na.
Success criteria: Six correct symbols with correct capitalisation; explanation mentions the Latin name natrium.

## Item 3: Indicator results

Outcomes: IS8.1.2.b
Item type: Data interpretation
Question: A learner added red cabbage indicator to four solutions. Solution A turned red, B turned purple, C turned green and D turned pink. Classify each solution as acidic, neutral or basic and name one household substance that could be solution C.
Success criteria: A and D acidic, B neutral, C basic; suggests soap, baking soda or wood ash solution for C.

## Item 4: Neutralisation in real life

Outcomes: IS8.1.2.c
Item type: Extended response
Question: A farmer in Kakamega finds that maize grows poorly because the soil is acidic. Explain what the farmer could add to the soil and why it works.
Success criteria: Names agricultural lime or another base; explains neutralisation of the acid; links to improved plant growth.
