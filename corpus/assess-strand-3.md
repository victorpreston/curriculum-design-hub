## Item 1: Drawing circuits

Outcomes: IS8.3.1.a
Item type: Diagram
Question: Using standard symbols, draw a circuit with one dry cell, a switch and two bulbs connected in parallel.
Success criteria: Correct symbols; two separate branches; switch placed in the main path.

## Item 2: Brightness and broken bulbs

Outcomes: IS8.3.1.b
Item type: Prediction and explanation
Question: Two identical bulbs are connected in series and two others in parallel, each pair to one dry cell. Predict which pair glows brighter and what happens in each circuit if one bulb blows.
Success criteria: Parallel bulbs brighter; in series both go off; in parallel the other stays on; explanation uses the idea of paths for current.

## Item 3: Hazard spotting

Outcomes: IS8.3.1.c
Item type: Scenario
Question: A learner sees a classmate holding a phone charger plugged into a socket with wet hands. List two dangers and describe what the learner should do.
Success criteria: Identifies risk of shock from water and from touching live parts; advises switching off at the socket and drying hands before touching electrical appliances.
