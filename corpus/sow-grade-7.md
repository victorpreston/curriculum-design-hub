## Lesson 1: Comparing cells

Week 2 · Outcomes: IS7.1.1.a
Lesson focus: Compare plant and animal cell structures.
Learning experiences: Learners label cell diagrams, identify structures shared by both cells and explain why a plant cell has additional structures.
Resources: Cell diagrams, hand lenses, prepared slides where available, exercise books.
Assessment: Labelled comparison table.
Safety: Handle slides carefully and wash hands after practical work.

## Lesson 2: Energy in appliances

Week 4 · Outcomes: IS7.2.1.a
Lesson focus: Trace energy changes in familiar devices.
Learning experiences: Groups examine pictures of appliances and draw arrows from the input energy to the useful output and any wasted energy.
Resources: Appliance picture cards, chart paper, markers.
Assessment: Energy-change diagram with an explanation.

## Lesson 3: Classifying changes

Week 6 · Outcomes: IS7.3.1.a
Lesson focus: Use evidence to classify changes.
Learning experiences: Learners observe safe demonstrations or picture sequences for melting, dissolving, rusting and cooking, then record signs of a new substance.
Resources: Ice, salt, water, picture cards, rusted metal sample.
Assessment: Classification table with evidence.
Safety: Teacher demonstrates heating or cooking; learners do not handle flames.
