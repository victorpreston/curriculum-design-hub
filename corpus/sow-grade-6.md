## Lesson 1: Separating mixtures

Week 3 · Outcomes: IS6.1.1.a
Lesson focus: Match separation methods to mixtures.
Learning experiences: Groups separate sand and water by filtration, salt and water by evaporation, and stones from sand by sieving. Learners explain why each method works.
Resources: Sand, water, salt, stones, sieves, funnels, filter paper, containers.
Assessment: Method selection table and explanation.
Safety: Keep work areas dry and wash hands after handling materials.

## Lesson 2: Building a balanced meal

Week 5 · Outcomes: IS6.2.1.a
Lesson focus: Use local foods to plan a balanced meal.
Learning experiences: Learners classify pictures of local foods by nutrient, then work in groups to design a balanced breakfast, lunch or supper.
Resources: Food picture cards, chart paper, markers.
Assessment: Meal plan with a reason for each food choice.

## Lesson 3: Investigating shadows

Week 7 · Outcomes: IS6.3.1.a
Lesson focus: Investigate how distance changes a shadow.
Learning experiences: Learners place an object between a torch and a screen, measure the shadow at two distances and share their observation.
Resources: Torches, cardboard shapes, white paper screens, rulers.
Assessment: Observation table and conclusion.
Safety: Never shine a torch directly into a learner's eyes.
