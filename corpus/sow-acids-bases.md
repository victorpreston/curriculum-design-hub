## Lesson 1: Making plant extract indicators

Week 3 · Outcomes: IS8.1.2.a
Lesson focus: Prepare indicators from locally available coloured plants.
Learning experiences: Groups crush red cabbage leaves, beetroot or hibiscus petals in a mortar, add warm water, stir and filter through a clean cloth into labelled bottles. Teacher emphasises that ethanol extracts more colour but must be kept away from flames. Learners record the colour of each extract.
Resources: Red cabbage, beetroot or hibiscus flowers, mortar and pestle, warm water, cloth or filter paper, labelled bottles, droppers.
Assessment: Observation checklist of correct extraction steps.
Safety: Do not taste any extract; wash hands after the activity.

## Lesson 2: Testing household substances

Week 3 · Outcomes: IS8.1.2.b
Lesson focus: Use indicators to classify substances as acidic, basic or neutral.
Learning experiences: Learners add three drops of plant extract indicator to small samples of lemon juice, vinegar, baking soda solution, soap solution, wood ash solution, salt solution and tap water. They repeat each test with red and blue litmus paper and record all colour changes in a results table, then classify each substance.
Resources: Plant extract indicators from Lesson 1, litmus paper, test tubes or clear plastic cups, household samples, droppers.
Assessment: Completed results table; classification accuracy.
Safety: Use small volumes; wood ash solution is caustic so avoid skin contact.

## Lesson 3: Uses of acids and bases

Week 4 · Outcomes: IS8.1.2.c
Lesson focus: Link everyday uses of acids and bases to their properties.
Learning experiences: Learners brainstorm where acids and bases are used at home, on the farm and in hospitals. Teacher introduces neutralisation using an antacid tablet in vinegar with indicator. Groups design a poster linking four uses to properties.
Resources: Antacid tablet, vinegar, indicator, chart paper.
Assessment: Poster rubric covering accuracy and explanation.

## Lesson 4: Neutralisation in the community

Week 4 · Outcomes: IS8.1.2.b, IS8.1.2.c
Lesson focus: Explain how farmers and health workers use neutralisation.
Learning experiences: Learners read a short case study of a farmer applying agricultural lime to acidic soil, test a soil sample with indicator, and discuss why bee stings are treated with a weak base and wasp stings with a weak acid.
Resources: Soil samples, indicator, case study handout.
Assessment: Short written explanation.
