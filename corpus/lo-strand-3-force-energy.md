## [IS8.3.1.a] Set up simple series and parallel electric circuits

Sub-strand: 3.1 Electrical Energy
Key inquiry question: How can we connect bulbs so that they all light up?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners use dry cells, bulbs, bulb holders, switches and connecting wires to set up circuits with two bulbs in series and two bulbs in parallel, and draw circuit diagrams using standard symbols.
Core competencies: Self-efficacy; Communication and collaboration.
Values: Responsibility in handling electrical components.

## [IS8.3.1.b] Compare the brightness of bulbs in series and parallel circuits

Sub-strand: 3.1 Electrical Energy
Key inquiry question: Why do bulbs in some circuits glow brighter than in others?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners observe bulb brightness in series and parallel arrangements, remove one bulb from each circuit to see what happens to the others, and explain why houses are wired in parallel.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Learning to learn.
Values: Integrity in reporting observations.

## [IS8.3.1.c] Observe safety precautions when handling electricity at home and in school

Sub-strand: 3.1 Electrical Energy
Key inquiry question: How can we prevent accidents caused by electricity?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners identify electrical hazards in pictures of homes and classrooms such as overloaded sockets, wet hands, bare wires and illegal connections, and role-play what to do when someone gets an electric shock.
Core competencies: Citizenship; Communication and collaboration.
Values: Responsibility and peace.
Pertinent and contemporary issues: Safety and security; Disaster risk reduction.
