## [IS5.1.1.a] Classify materials as solids, liquids or gases

Sub-strand: 5.1 Matter around us
Key inquiry question: How can we describe materials by observing their properties?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners sort familiar materials into solids, liquids and gases, then explain the property used for each choice.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Communication and collaboration.
Values: Respect for shared materials; Responsibility.

## [IS5.2.1.a] Identify basic needs of plants and animals

Sub-strand: 5.2 Living things
Key inquiry question: What do plants and animals need to stay alive?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners observe plants and discuss how air, water, light, food and shelter support living things.
Core competencies: Observation; Communication and collaboration.
Values: Care for living things; Unity.

## [IS5.3.1.a] Describe how pushes and pulls change movement

Sub-strand: 5.3 Forces in everyday life
Key inquiry question: How can a push or pull change the movement of an object?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners push toy cars and pull objects with strings, recording changes in direction, speed and position.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Creativity and imagination.
Values: Safety; Responsibility.
