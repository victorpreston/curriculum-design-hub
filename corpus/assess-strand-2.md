## Item 1: Cell parts and functions

Outcomes: IS8.2.1.a
Item type: Matching
Question: Match each cell part to its function: nucleus, cell membrane, chloroplast, cell wall, vacuole. Functions: controls the activities of the cell; makes food using light; gives the plant cell a fixed shape; controls what enters and leaves the cell; stores cell sap.
Success criteria: All five matched correctly.

## Item 2: Plant versus animal cells

Outcomes: IS8.2.1.b
Item type: Comparison table
Question: State three differences between a plant cell and an animal cell and explain why plant cells need a cell wall.
Success criteria: Mentions cell wall, chloroplasts and large permanent vacuole; explanation refers to support and fixed shape.

## Item 3: Explaining diffusion

Outcomes: IS8.2.2.a
Item type: Short answer
Question: When food is being cooked in the school kitchen, learners in a classroom nearby can smell it. Use the idea of diffusion to explain why.
Success criteria: Describes particles moving from high to low concentration; uses the term diffusion correctly.

## Item 4: Osmosis investigation

Outcomes: IS8.2.2.b
Item type: Practical data interpretation
Question: Potato strip A was placed in water and grew from 50 mm to 54 mm. Strip B was placed in salt solution and shrank from 50 mm to 46 mm. Explain both results.
Success criteria: A gained water by osmosis because the water outside was more dilute; B lost water because the salt solution was more concentrated; mentions the partially permeable membrane.
Rubric: Exceeds expectations explains both with the membrane; Meets expectations explains both; Approaches expectations explains one; Below expectations describes without explaining.
