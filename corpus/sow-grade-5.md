## Lesson 1: Sorting materials

Week 2 · Outcomes: IS5.1.1.a
Lesson focus: Classify familiar materials by state.
Learning experiences: Groups sort water, stones, cooking oil, air in a balloon and books into solids, liquids and gases, then justify their decisions.
Resources: Clear cups, stones, cooking oil, balloons, books, sorting cards.
Assessment: Sorting record and oral explanations.
Safety: Do not taste classroom materials; keep liquids away from electrical equipment.

## Lesson 2: Needs of living things

Week 4 · Outcomes: IS5.2.1.a
Lesson focus: Connect basic needs to plant and animal survival.
Learning experiences: Learners examine a healthy plant, compare it with a wilted plant and create a needs wheel for plants and animals.
Resources: Two plants, picture cards, chart paper, pencils.
Assessment: Needs wheel with one reason for each need.

## Lesson 3: Pushes and pulls

Week 6 · Outcomes: IS5.3.1.a
Lesson focus: Observe how forces change movement.
Learning experiences: Pairs push toy cars over different surfaces and pull objects with a string, recording which force changes speed or direction.
Resources: Toy cars, string, books, smooth and rough surfaces.
Assessment: Investigation table and learner explanation.
Safety: Keep objects on the floor or table and avoid pulling towards another learner.
