## [IS6.1.1.a] Separate mixtures using suitable methods

Sub-strand: 6.1 Mixtures
Key inquiry question: Which method can separate the materials in a mixture?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners separate sand and water by filtration, salt and water by evaporation, and stones and sand by sieving.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Creativity and imagination.
Values: Integrity when recording results; Responsibility.

## [IS6.2.1.a] Explain the importance of a balanced diet

Sub-strand: 6.2 Nutrition
Key inquiry question: How does the food we choose support a healthy body?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners group local foods by nutrients and design a balanced meal using affordable foods available in their community.
Core competencies: Communication and collaboration; Self-efficacy.
Values: Healthy living; Respect for different food cultures.

## [IS6.3.1.a] Describe how light forms shadows

Sub-strand: 6.3 Light
Key inquiry question: What conditions are needed for a shadow to form?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners investigate a torch, an opaque object and a screen, changing distances and observing the size of the shadow.
Core competencies: Observation; Critical thinking and problem solving.
Values: Curiosity; Cooperation.
