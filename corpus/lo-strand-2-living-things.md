## [IS8.2.1.a] Describe the functions of the main parts of plant and animal cells

Sub-strand: 2.1 The Cell
Key inquiry question: How does each part of a cell help it to stay alive?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners study labelled charts or micrographs of plant and animal cells, identify the cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, cell wall, chloroplast and vacuole, and match each part to its function.
Core competencies: Learning to learn; Critical thinking and problem solving.
Values: Respect for life.

## [IS8.2.1.b] Compare plant and animal cells using models or micrographs

Sub-strand: 2.1 The Cell
Key inquiry question: In what ways are plant cells different from animal cells?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners build simple cell models using locally available materials such as clear polythene bags, porridge or gelatine, seeds and beans, then use their models to compare plant and animal cells in a table.
Core competencies: Creativity and imagination; Communication and collaboration.
Values: Unity and responsibility when sharing materials.
Pertinent and contemporary issues: Waste management when reusing materials for models.

## [IS8.2.2.a] Demonstrate diffusion using everyday materials

Sub-strand: 2.2 Movement of Materials In and Out of the Cell
Key inquiry question: How do substances spread from one place to another?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners observe a drop of food colour or potassium manganate(VII) crystal in still water, and the spread of a perfume or burning incense smell across the classroom, then explain diffusion as movement from a region of high concentration to low concentration.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Self-efficacy.
Values: Responsibility in handling chemicals safely.

## [IS8.2.2.b] Investigate osmosis using plant tissue

Sub-strand: 2.2 Movement of Materials In and Out of the Cell
Key inquiry question: What happens to plant cells placed in water and in salty water?
Suggested learning experiences: Learners place equal potato or arrow root strips in plain water and in concentrated salt solution, measure their lengths and feel their firmness after thirty minutes, and explain the changes in terms of water moving across a partially permeable membrane.
Core competencies: Critical thinking and problem solving; Learning to learn.
Values: Integrity when measuring and recording results.
Pertinent and contemporary issues: Food preservation using salt and sugar.
